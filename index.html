🌟 Super Challenge: Spot the Ad

🤔 Is it good that apps choose videos for us? Why or why not?
Good: we find things we like faster. Not so good: we may see only one kind of thing and watch for too long.
🇪🇺 Digital Services Act
❓ Should apps tell you WHY they recommend something?
Yes! In Europe, big apps must explain how their recommendations work and let people choose a feed that is not based on their personal history.
⏰ The next video plays by itself (autoplay). How can we stop watching too much?
Turn off autoplay, set a timer, decide before you start how many videos you will watch.
